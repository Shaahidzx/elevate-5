What is ELEVATE?

ELEVATE is the flagship leadership camp of AIESEC in NSBM, now in its fifth year. A selected group of undergraduates leaves the lecture halls behind and spends days together learning what leading actually feels like.

AIESEC is the world's largest youth-run organisation, in over 100 countries. ELEVATE is where our chapter brings that spirit home, with workshops, challenges, company partners and a great deal of candlelight.
